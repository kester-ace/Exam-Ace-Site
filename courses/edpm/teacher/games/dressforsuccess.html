Dress code guide

Business attire is part of your professional image. Good grooming — maintaining appropriate personal presentation suited to a professional environment — is one of the desirable habits and work attitudes employers look for.

Colour rule: build on neutrals (navy, charcoal, black, white, cream, light blue, khaki) and add at most one accent (burgundy or forest green). Avoid neon, loud prints and clashing colours.

Health & safety: closed-toe, low-heeled shoes protect your feet and reduce slips, trips and falls.

Respect for diversity: cultural and religious head coverings, such as a headwrap, are always welcome at work.
