Description of Components

This portfolio contains nine samples of documents, each prepared with the software named below.

1. Ruled tabulation using spreadsheet software: A ruled table of excursion bookings for Frigatebird Island Excursions Ltd from October to December 2025. It has a merged main heading, two levels of column headings set at oblique and vertical angles, a harmonious teal colour scheme, records sorted in ascending order by excursion and totals calculated with the SUM function. A formula view follows. Software: Microsoft Excel.

2. Financial document using spreadsheet software: An income and expenditure statement for the Calabash Youth Steel Orchestra for the quarter ended 31 December 2025. It uses merged, bold headings, borders, oblique and vertical column headings, items sorted from A to Z, the SUM and MAX functions and currency formatting to two decimal places. A formula view follows. Software: Microsoft Excel.

3. Committee documents: A notice of a meeting of the Executive Committee of the Sea Grape Arts Collective, with its agenda. The notice states the place, date and time, the members who should attend and the convenor; the agenda has the seven standard items, with New Business sub-numbered. Software: Microsoft Word.

4. Legal document: A six-month residential lease between Hibiscus Hill Properties Ltd and its tenant. It identifies both parties, sets out nine numbered clauses, includes a schedule of payments that agrees with the clauses and provides signature spaces for the landlord, the tenant and a witness. Software: Microsoft Word.

5. Advanced display: organisational chartAdvanced display: flow chart: An organisational chart of Frigatebird Island Excursions Ltd showing sixteen positions on four levels and the lines of authority between them, displayed on landscape paper with narrow margins. Software: Microsoft Word (SmartArt Hierarchy layout).A flow chart of the booking process at Frigatebird Island Excursions Ltd, drawn with the standard terminator, process, decision and input/output symbols joined by labelled flow lines, and displayed on landscape paper with narrow margins. Software: Microsoft Word (Shapes).

6. Simple display: invitation with a menu: A four-page booklet for the tenth anniversary dinner of Frigatebird Island Excursions Ltd, printed on both sides of one A4 sheet and folded. It contains the invitation with R.S.V.P. details and the menu, framed by an art border. Software: Microsoft Word (Book Fold layout).

7. Mail merge: Letters from the Sea Grape Arts Collective confirming stands at its Art on the Quay exhibition. The main document has merge fields in the inside address, salutation and body; the data source holds eight records; the eight merged letters follow, one to a page. Software: Microsoft Word (Mailings) and Microsoft Excel.

8. Database: A database of the artworks entered for Art on the Quay. It shows the table design (field names, data types, field sizes and primary key), a datasheet of fourteen records, a simple query, a complex query with two criteria and a report grouped by medium. Software: Microsoft Access.

9. Presentation: A nine-slide presentation to accompany our group research paper, “Paper to Digital: How Small Businesses in Antigua Manage Their Documents”. It has a title slide, an introduction, a body, a conclusion and a references slide, with charts, images, transitions, animations and speaker notes. Software: Microsoft PowerPoint.

THIS LEASE AGREEMENT (the “Agreement”) is made on 1 September 2025

BETWEEN HIBISCUS HILL PROPERTIES LTD, a property management company with its office at 3 Friars Hill Road, St. John’s, Antigua, represented by its Managing Director, Mrs. Monique Hassell (the “Landlord”),

AND MS. ADRIENNE FELIX, Accountant, employed by Frigatebird Island Excursions Ltd, of 8 Orange Grove Road, Cedar Grove, Antigua (the “Tenant”).

NOW THEREFORE, in consideration of the covenants and obligations contained in this Agreement, the parties agree as follows:

  1. PROPERTY. The Landlord agrees to let and the Tenant agrees to rent the furnished one-bedroom apartment known as Apartment 4, Palm Court, 25 Old Parham Road, St. John’s, Antigua, together with one parking space (the “Property”).
  2. LEASE TERM. This lease is for a fixed term of six (6) months, commencing on 1 September 2025 and ending on 28 February 2026 (the “Lease Term”). At the end of the Lease Term, the Tenant shall vacate the Property and return all keys to the Landlord, unless a new agreement has been signed by both parties.
  3. RENT. The Tenant shall pay rent of two thousand five hundred Eastern Caribbean dollars (EC$2,500.00) per month, in advance, on the first (1st) day of each month, by bank transfer to the account named by the Landlord. The first month’s rent is due on the signing of this Agreement.
  4. LATE RENT. If rent is not received within five (5) days of the due date, a late fee of one hundred Eastern Caribbean dollars (EC$100.00) shall be charged for each occurrence.
  5. SECURITY DEPOSIT. On the signing of this Agreement, the Tenant shall pay a security deposit of two thousand five hundred Eastern Caribbean dollars (EC$2,500.00). The Landlord may deduct from the deposit any unpaid rent, late fees, unpaid utilities and the cost of repairing damage beyond normal wear and tear. The balance shall be returned to the Tenant within thirty (30) days after the end of the Lease Term.

  • Boxes of different sizes on the same level, or connecting lines that cross or stop short of a box.
