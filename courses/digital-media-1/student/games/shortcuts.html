Faster fingers, smarter work

Shortcut keys combine a modifier key — such as Ctrl or Alt — with another key to perform a command instantly. Learn them, sprint against the clock, match them up, then spot how the same keys do different jobs in Word, Excel, PowerPoint and Access.

Play with a real keyboard or tap the on-screen keys. On-screen Ctrl, Shift, Alt and Win stay held down (latched) until you tap the next key. Shortcuts marked with a gold chip come from your Chapter 2 notes.
