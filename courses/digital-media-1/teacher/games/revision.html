This activity trains you to evaluate exam answers. Read each case study carefully, then decide how CXC/CAPE would likely view the answer.

How to judge answers

Correct: accurate, specific, linked to the case, and answers what was asked.

Partially Correct: has some correct ideas, but is vague, incomplete, weakly linked, or slightly confused.

Incorrect: wrong concept, off-topic, unrealistic, or does not answer the question.
