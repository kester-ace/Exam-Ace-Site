Digital Media Unit 1 Mark Scheme

Paper 031 School-Based Assessment · 45 marks (weighted to 135 by CXC) · Marking sheet

Criteria from the Unit 1 School-Based Assessment requirements of the CXC CAPE Digital Media syllabus: Research/Pitch 10, Progress and Process 15, Proposal 20. The pitch is a 5-minute oral presentation and the report is 1,000 to 1,500 words. Each module receives one third of the total (remainder 1 to Module 3; remainder 2 to Modules 3 and 2). Weighting: this sheet weights the score to 135, as the syllabus assessment grid does for the SBA (60% of the unit). The Unit 1 marking criteria page of the syllabus prints “weighted by CXC to 90”; the assessment grid gives 135.
