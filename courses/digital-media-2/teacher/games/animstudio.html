Timing and Spacing Charts

Animators plan motion with a spacing chart: a line between two keys with a tick for every in-between. Wide gaps mean fast; tight gaps mean slow. Match each chart to the motion.

Teacher's tip: Timing is how many frames an action takes. Spacing is where each drawing sits within that time. The same 12 frames can feel heavy or light depending on spacing alone.

Walk Cycle Builder

A walk step has four key poses. Put them in order, pose each one with the sliders, then play the loop. The second step is mirrored for you (legs and arms swap), so one good step gives a full cycle.

    3D Concepts Check

    Before Malik moves the bouncing ball into Blender, make sure you can name the parts of a mesh and the tools for keyframing and tweening in 3D.

    Mesh explorer

    A vertex is a point in 3D space. An edge joins two vertices. A face is a flat polygon enclosed by edges. A cube primitive is 8 vertices, 12 edges and 6 faces. Drag the cube to turn it.
