Funding a Digital Media Project: Highlight Activity

Topic: Ways to fund a digital media project

Instructions: Read each category below. Select text that describes a benefit and click Benefit (Green). Select text that describes a disadvantage and click Disadvantage (Red). Each category includes three lines (overview, at least one benefit, and at least one disadvantage). On a phone, tap a line to select it, then press a button. When you finish, press Check my highlights.

Tip: Drag to select text inside the articles, then press a button. Use your browser’s Undo (Ctrl/Cmd+Z) if you make a mistake.
