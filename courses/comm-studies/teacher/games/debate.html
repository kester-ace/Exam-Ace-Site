Power Move

PEEL every point: Point, Evidence, Explain, Link back to the motion.

  1. Unpack the motionWho is affected? What exactly changes? Define the key words before the other team does.
  2. Pick three pointsChoose your strongest. Add one real example for each, ideally from your own world.
  3. Plan the Power MoveDecide which moment of your speech it lands in, and who delivers it.
