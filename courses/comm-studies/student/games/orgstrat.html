Extract 2 — Hurricane Irma

"When Hurricane Irma struck Sint Maarten in September 2017, the destruction was staggering. Roofs were torn from homes, boats were flung onto roads, and the island's main power grid was crippled for months. As a direct result, thousands of residents were left without electricity, businesses were forced to close, and the tourism season that had been the island's lifeline came to an abrupt halt."

Extract 3 — Cruise Tourism Debate

"Some residents argue that Saint Lucia should scale back cruise tourism to reduce environmental strain on the island's beaches and reefs. It is true that cruise ships bring large volumes of visitors in short bursts, straining local infrastructure. However, cruise tourism also generates substantial revenue for local vendors, taxi drivers, and small businesses who depend on ship-day traffic to survive; scaling it back without a viable alternative would devastate this segment of the workforce."
